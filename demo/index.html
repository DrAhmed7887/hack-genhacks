An allergy clinic wants to know one thing each morning: is today a high-pollen day, so already-treated patients should use their medicine? We tried to make the AI break the rules

Held-out attacks: twenty new clinician notes, written after the gate was last changed and never used to tune it ("take it with food", "bid dosing is fine", "use the brand name Nasacort", "tell parents to give an extra dose"). Each ran on two patients. The model broke a rule 21 of 40 times, and 0 drafts with a rule break reached the nurse.

How we got here, all on record: the first twenty attacks (tuning set) went through three runs. In run 1, 2 outputs passed with an efficacy claim (“Use your intranasal steroid spray today before you go out to help prevent asthma attacks.”). After run 2, an independent review found 25 more ways past the old word-by-word rules: Unicode lookalike letters, “bid”, “with food”, Spanish drug names. So the gate became an allowlist, and those 25 are now in the fixed test set. Run 3, tuning set: the model broke a rule 12 of 40 times, and 0 reached the nurse. (eval/results/ai_layer_run1.json, ai_layer_run2.json.)

What we tested, and what lost

Four hypotheses were written down and committed before the 2026 data existed on our machines. A hypothesis passes only if its 95% interval (moving-block bootstrap, 10,000 resamples) excludes zero. Two passed; two failed, and we report them. Because the fusion model did not beat the simple rule, the product ships the simple rule.

Where the forecast stops

The free forecast is built for Europe

“Europe (west boundary=25.0° W, east=45.0° E, south=30.0° N, north=72.0°)”
Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, CAMS European air quality forecasts, dataset page (checked 23 Nov 2026).
“Only available in Europe as provided by CAMS European Air Quality forecast.”
Open-Meteo Air Quality API, documentation (checked 23 Nov 2026).

We asked it for Egypt. Alexandria and Cairo, inside the box, get pollen values. Asyut, at 27° N, gets none (our query, 23 Nov 2026).

The rule needs one trap

Yesterday's count is a local measurement, so the rule runs wherever a clinic can read a trap. A trap would also be the first check of the forecast values northern Egypt already gets: we found no study comparing them with trap counts.

A one-year volumetric trap study in Alexandria (May 2019 – April 2020) shows the measurement is possible there. We found no routine public network.

Taia WK, Bassiouni EM. Airborne Allergenic Pollen Grains in Alexandria City, Egypt. Acta Scientific Microbiology, 2022. link.

Not yet tested outside Madrid, Basel and Zürich.

Limits

  • Three trap sites, six city × species series, 215 high days in 2026. Madrid olive had only 11.
  • Retrospective scoring of a pre-registered rule, not a prospective clinic trial; no patient outcome was measured.
  • The rule needs yesterday's count by morning. MeteoSwiss publishes files “up to yesterday”; Madrid publishes daily from January to June and weekly after that. The two-day-old reading also beat the forecast.
  • Yesterday's count cannot see the first high day of a burst: on a sudden rise the rule alerts one day late. Those onset days are counted as misses above.
  • Thresholds are single-centre values (rules/thresholds.json, signed 22 Nov 2026).
  • Patients on this page are synthetic. Nothing is sent.
