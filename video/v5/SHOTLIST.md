## 1. Design concept: the tape

A pollen trap records the air onto a strip of sticky tape, one band per day. The whole film is that tape. Every data scene is a strip on warm paper: bars for counts, boxes for days, dots for events, so the viewer learns one grammar once (filled = flagged, hollow = missed) and reads every later scene without effort. The trap is the only 3D object and it appears once, at the moment we say "the clinic's own pollen trap", and its tape unrolls into the flat strip the film then keeps using. No dark void anywhere: paper `#F6F4EE`, ink `#15201B`. Green `#16794A` means trap and nothing else. Amber `#B4570B` means forecast and nothing else. Muted red `#A33A2F` appears only for "missed" and "Failed". Camera moves are rare (three in the whole film); most scenes are locked frames in which numbers arrive on their spoken cue and then stay still.

Global rules (Remotion, 1920x1080, 30 fps):
- One non-overlapping `<Sequence>` per scene (10 sequences, `from` = running sum of durations, no overlap, no gaps).
- Root-level `<Captions>` outside all Sequences, driven by the approved script text split on the alignment JSON (never ASR text). Burned-in: ON (first viewing is muted). 36 px Inter 600, ink on a paper pill, centred, baseline at y=1010. Every layout keeps y>960 free.
- Root-level progress bar: 4 px, ink, y=1076, full width scaled by frame/total. Ink, not green (green is reserved).
- Transitions: hard cuts by default, because a dissolve between two light paper frames reads as a mistake. Continuity is done by design: the outgoing element is already where the incoming one starts (match cuts, listed in the last column). No crossfades, no wipes, no zoom-blurs.
- Capture crops: every crop is scaled to at least 1.1x its native px, so css 14 px text lands at >=31 px at 1080p. Crops that must be zoomed further are noted per shot.
- Voice: ElevenLabs Daniel, 1.08. Numbers are announced on-screen on the spoken word, not before.

## 3. 3D shot specs (total 3D = 7 s of the 25 s budget)

Where 3D earns its place: one place only. The trap is the film's subject and the evidence's source; the audience has never seen one and cannot guess how "yesterday's count" exists. Scene 2 says it the moment the narration says it. Everything else is stronger flat (data = strips; the CAMS map must be an honest line drawing).

**3D-A: Hirst-type volumetric trap (scene 2a, 0:11-0:18)**
- Geometry (all procedural, no assets, no AI imagery): a short mast; a body with a front intake orifice (slit nozzle), a wind vane on a rear tail so the orifice faces upwind; a cutaway through the body wall showing a horizontal rotating drum; the drum is wrapped in a strip of sticky tape with day ticks every 1/7 of the circumference; a thin clock motor pad. Matte clay shading, ink edge via Freestyle, paper ground, warm area light from top left. Grains on the tape: 4,000 icosphere instances, 1 pore dimple each (grass-type grain: near-spherical, single pore), ochre `#B9A97A`, jittered radius +-15%, with a scatter weighted to look like deposit, not decoration. No labels on the geometry.
- Camera: 210 frames at 30 fps. Frames 0-80: slow dolly in from a 3/4 wide to the cutaway window (Camera move: not counted as a Remotion move; it is part of the 3D). Frames 80-150: drum rotates 1/7 turn, tape peels off the drum and unrolls to the right as a flat strip lying on the paper plane. Frames 150-210: macro push to the tape surface, depth of field on, grains in focus at the centre; hold the last 12 frames locked.
- The last frame's tape strip must sit at the exact position, height and colour of the Remotion strip of 2b (strip top y=620, height 160 px, `#D8D3C6`) so the cut is invisible. Alpha not needed; the paper ground is rendered in.
- Render: Blender Cycles on GPU, 1920x1080, 96 samples + OIDN denoise, filmic off (Standard view transform) so paper `#F6F4EE` stays exact, 210 frames to PNG. Per the storage rules this goes to Colab first (400 units available, free), never a paid service. No render before Ahmed approves the style frames.
- Why it earns its place: it shows where the count comes from (air through an orifice onto tape, counted by hand) in 7 s, and its unrolled tape becomes the strip that carries the rest of the film.

Rejected 3D, so nobody revives them: standalone pollen grain macro (pretty, adds no claim), 2026 season as a 3D landscape (a real chart does it better and it was the V4 mistake in another form), globe with the CAMS box (the brief requires a plain line drawing; a 3D globe invites "works in Egypt"), cylinders of any kind.
